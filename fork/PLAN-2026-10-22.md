# MrSurvivor: repeat runs, longer runs, move diversity, Kecleon integration, workflow

Planning document, 2026-10-22. No code changed. Built from six analyses made in the Claude session of
that day (move mechanics, run structure, upstream-vs-Lab integration, genre research, community
feedback, session history). Line numbers are from commit 529f119 / merge c1bdc5d. Section G records
the owner's answers (2026-10-22); every decision is made and the sections below are updated to
match. Next step: turn this file into the work list (`fork/TODO.md`, see E2).

Everything proposed goes in `js/fork/**` as wrappers of functions the game already calls as `G.X.y`
(the pattern of `rift-scaling.js`, `eternatus.js`, `prestige.js`), Lab first unless marked, each item
its own commit with a `fork/test-*.cjs` check, measured with `fork/survival-lab.cjs` and
`fork/balance-lab.cjs` where damage or survival changes.

## 0. The six root causes

1. **Mobility and close-range moves fight the player.** Dash, recoil, leap and roll auto-fire the
   moment a target is in reach, aim at *whatever is touching you* (`player.js:461-481`: threat >
   boss > nearest > rock/chest), and move you further per cooldown than you can walk (dash 110-120 px
   every 0.55-1.3 s vs ~80 px of walking). A dash runs its **full radius** past the target into the
   pack behind it (`combat.js:340-349`); a leap lands **on** the enemy and its invulnerability ends
   0.15 s later (`kinds.js:160-175`). The 0.3 s of i-frames don't cover what kills close players:
   hazards and lava zero `invuln` before hitting (`hazards.js:50`, `player.js:445`) and every mover
   passes `allowLiquid = true`. Spin, dash and recoil get **no** 25 % close-range cut; only melee,
   grab, bind, focus and ramp do (`player.js:305`, `kinds2.js:49`). Ramp stacks reset on a 1.5 s
   gap, i.e. on kiting. Melee idles when a boss enters 330 px but isn't in reach (upstream #186,
   #208). The survival bot already quantifies it: close builds last 101-157 s vs 200 s for Aura
   Sphere alone (`fork/LAB.md` Phase 9).

2. **Moves are kind templates.** 74 of 103 upstream moves differ from their kind only in numbers; 26
   have no extra field at all (not even knockback). Status carriers: 27 of 103. The one ladder per
   kind (`moves.js:40-179`) makes every move of a kind converge at level 5 (every melee becomes a
   360° ×2.1 swing; Ember and Thunder Shock both become 3-shot fans). Evolution is per kind, numbers
   plus a rename; seven evolved names promise mechanics they don't have (Future Sight, Stealth Rock,
   Venoshock, Belly Drum, Avalanche, Endeavor, Tailwind). Lab supports per-move `ups`/`evoMod`
   (`lab.js:37-42`, `kinds2.js:89`) but only Protect, Rest and Shell Trap use it.

3. **The second half of a run is empty and the hand goes stale.** Boss slots end at 15:00
   (`enemies.js:44-50`); no new enemy enters the roster after 8:20; the spawn cap (64) binds from
   ~minute 8 so the rate formula is decorative. XP per kill has no time term (`enemies.js:177`,
   `spawner.js:107`) while HP grows ×(1 + t/95), so XP/s decays ~1/t after minute 8, exactly when
   hands have already collapsed to Vigor / Potencia / Síntesis / Baya. Every choice has a dominant
   answer (own-type strong-side move, the gold evo card, fuse the bond, Choice Band, Potencia).

4. **The end is a wall with no shape.** Normal: Arceus at 30:00, nothing survives past ~40:00 by
   design (`judgment.js:36-45`). Lab: beat Eternatus and the *same* linear curve continues with no
   bosses, no new roster, no regime change. Nothing after 30:00 rewards anything.

5. **The meta pushes one Pokémon to exactly 30:00.** 40,800 coins to max a Pokémon, per Pokémon,
   at 2,000-4,500 coins a run; nothing rewards a different build or type; the rainbow ticket lands at
   30:00 and never again; leaderboards rank a time compressed to seconds for everyone who reaches
   Arceus.

6. **Two ability engines, one nature lost.** In a Lab run `game.js:190` sets upstream's ability and
   `lab.js:204` attaches Lab's: for 648 species both fire and stack (Blaze ×1.5 × ×1.35 = ×2.0,
   Thick Fat ×0.5 × ×0.6 = ×0.3, two Sturdies, Static slows *and* stuns). A Lab evolution replaces
   `p.mon` with the raw dex entry (`growth.js:87,92`), so the nature vanishes and the ability is
   never re-resolved. TMs you bought never appear in Lab runs (`labPool` ignores `G.Mons.taught`).

## A. Close range and mobility

Goal: a close-range kit lasts at least as long as the best ranged kit in `survival-lab.cjs`, and a
mobility move never moves the player somewhere they didn't choose.

### A1. Tuning that needs no decision (ship first, Lab)

| # | Change | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1.1 | Spin, dash and recoil join the 25 % close-range cut | add to `CLOSE` in `kinds2.js:49`; publish the `close: Rapid Spin` survival row | doofcraft did the same for spin (v29); today they are the only close kinds without it |
| A1.2 | Relocation grace: 0.5 s after any dash/leap/recoil/roll that hazards can't bypass | a `graceT` like `revive-grace.js:139-146` checked in a `Player.hurt` wrapper | hazards and lava zero `invuln` first; the revive-grace pattern already proves the fix |
| A1.3 | Leap invuln `dur + 0.6`, blast on landing | `kinds.js:164` | 0.15 s after landing in the middle of the crowd is nothing |
| A1.4 | Recoil: cap self-damage per second (8 % max HP/s) instead of 12 % per cast; refund half the recoil on a KO | `kinds.js:229-236` | against a crowd every cast hits the cap: ~10 % HP/s on top of contact damage |
| A1.5 | Ramp stacks decay one per 1.5 s instead of resetting; roll approach capped at 30 px and skipped when you walk away from the target | `kinds.js:245-251, 266` | Fury Cutter lasted 78 → 111 s; kiting is the genre's core loop and ramp punishes it |
| A1.6 | Melee targeting: aim at the nearest enemy *in reach*; boss priority only when the boss is in reach | wrap the target pick for `melee`/`spin`/`grab`/`bind` kinds (`player.js:466`) | fixes the "stand still until the boss is in range" freeze (#186, #208) |
| A1.7 | Movers never fire at rocks or chests | skip the cast when `pl.target` has no `hurt` (`combat.js:38`, as `chain` does at `:269`) | a dash at a rock drags you across the map |

### A2. Control: who decides where you go (decided: policy (a) + A2.4, see G1-G2)

Three candidate policies were considered, all fork-side wrappers of `Combat.run` for the mover
kinds. **Chosen: (a).** (b) and (c) are not built.

- **(a) Movement-directed, return to start (CHOSEN).** If you are walking, the dash goes in
  your movement direction (`pl.moveAngle`), else toward the aim; it hits everything along the
  line and **puts you back where you started** (the Whip/Knife rule from Vampire Survivors: the
  weapon is directional, the player is never displaced). Fires only when an enemy lies along that
  line within reach. Camera kick removed. Co-op: nothing changes (`if (ghost) return` already
  precedes the position write).
- **(b) Fire only when standing still** (upstream PR #296, open). Simple, no overshoot into chests
  or rifts, but a dash user who never stops deals no damage, and it does nothing for landing in the
  pack.
- **(c) Steer around obstacles or stay put** (doofcraft v30). Fixes getting stuck on trees, not the
  core complaint.

Plus, independent of the policy: **A2.4 a "Mobility moves fire by hand" setting** (decided: add
it, off by default; Settings → Accessibility): dash/leap/recoil/roll only go off while the fire button is held,
reusing `ManualFire`'s clock-hold (`manual-fire.js:84-86`) restricted to a `MOBILE` kind set.
Everything else keeps auto-firing. It turns a mobility move into a decision without changing anyone
else's game.

### A3. Identity for the moves themselves (Lab, per-move `ups`/`evoMod`)

- **Pivots.** U-turn, Volt Switch, Flip Turn, Parting Shot: hit, return to start, `pl.cycle(1)` to
  the next slot ("hit and swap"). Today U-turn is a plain dash. Rewards slot switching, which is the
  game's signature mechanic.
- **Flame Charge** becomes a short dash *plus* a speed buff stack; Quick Attack / Extreme Speed /
  Aqua Step stay dashes under policy (a).
- **Leaps.** Fly and Bounce stay at your position and drop the blast on the target (you are
  untouchable while "in the air"); Dig/Dive land *beside* the target (−70 px toward you); Phantom
  Force lands behind you; Shadow Force keeps its 3-hop chain (already untouchable throughout).
- **Melee.** Give the 11 upstream melee moves one trait each from fields that already exist:
  reach (radius 90+, narrow arc: Vine Whip, Drill Peck), heavy knock + 0.3 s `flinch` (Body Slam,
  Stomp), `leech` (Drain Punch, Horn Leech, Leech Life), parry (`guard` on Counter-like evolutions),
  360° only on the moves whose name says so (Petal Dance, Rapid Spin).
- **Counter / Mirror Coat / Metal Burst** as the "Aegis" pattern: block, then a shockwave scaled by
  what was blocked. Exists in Lab (`guard` kind); promote once A1 lands.
- **Stance (optional, later).** A toggling buff slot: Guard (+25 % reduction, −20 % damage) / Rush
  (+20 % speed, dashes return to start). Read in `Player.hurt`/`atkFor` wrappers.

### A4. Measure

Add a `BUILDS` row per change in `fork/survival-lab.cjs:30-49`, 6 runs each (±20-30 s noise), and
compare with `far: Aura Sphere` (200 s). Target after A1-A3: `close: Karate Chop` ≥ 200 s,
`close: Quick Attack` ≥ 180 s, no close build under 150 s. Rerun `fork/balance-lab.cjs` and keep
the ×kind of dashes/leaps between 0.9 and 1.3.

**Promotion (decided, G12):** once these targets are met, the Phase 9 close-range kit and the
spin/dash/recoil close-range cut go to normal runs without asking again. Record the survival-lab
numbers in the commit message.

## B. Move diversity

Goal: two moves of the same kind feel different at level 1, and more so at level 5.

### B1. Per-move ladders and honest evolutions (Lab, mechanism exists)

- Give every move whose evolved name lies the mechanic it promises: **Future Sight** (mark, see
  B3.1), **Stealth Rock** (persistent field, B3.2), **Venoshock** (poison converter, B3.3),
  **Belly Drum** (Fillet Away's `drum`, `kinds2.js:447`), **Avalanche** (×2 if hit in the last
  1.5 s, `revenge`), **Endeavor** (HP-state, B3.12), **Tailwind** (speed buff + shove).
- The 26 "no extra field" moves get one field each (knock, pierce, slow, burn, homing, or a
  behavioural `ups` step). Start with the five spins and four shoves, which are literally
  interchangeable today.
- Per-kind ladders stay as the default; a move's own `ups` overrides steps. Behavioural steps for
  movers and melee: "stops at the first enemy", "returns to start", "leaves a trail",
  "+0.5 s grace".

### B2. Branching upgrades (Lab)

At level 3 the hand offers two cards for the same move (`up:id:a` / `up:id:b`, stored as
`m.branch`), e.g. Flamethrower → Fire Spin (bind) or Heat Wave (fan); Thunderbolt → Volt Switch
(pivot) or Discharge (aura). Fork wrappers of `G.Moves.nextUpText/levelUp` and `G.LevelUp.offer`
(`levelup.js:108-118`). Halls of Torment's rule: at most 2 of 3 branches per run.

### B3. New mechanics (none present today; each maps to a hook)

### B4. A synergy layer (the thing that makes a *build*)

- **Set bonuses** (Brotato): 3 moves of one type in your slots → that type's effect (Fire: +1 burn
  stack; Water: slow; Electric: chain hop; Grass: leech; Fighting: +20 % physical and the close
  cut everywhere); 3 physical → lifesteal; 2 priority moves → faster switching. Wrap
  `Player.prototype.atkFor` / `addMove` (as `growth.js:258-264`), chip via `G.HUD.belowHp`.
- **Synergy cards** (20 Minutes Till Dawn): red-bordered cards that only exist once 2-3
  prerequisites are owned (Ice + Fire moves → Frost Fire: frozen enemies also burn; Thunder Wave +
  an Electric attack → Overload: hitting a paralysed enemy explodes). Via `G.LevelUp.offer`.
- **Bond recipes** (Vampire Survivors unions): specific pairs change behaviour, not just count
  (Sunny Day + Solar Beam skips the charge; Rain Dance + Thunder never misses and chains).
- **Rare-card family** beside Second Nature (1/300-1/500 via `G.RareCards.maybe`,
  `levelup.js:159`): Twin Focus (two active slots fire, −20 % damage), Echo (active move recasts at
  60 % after 0.4 s), Glass Cannon (×1.5 damage, −30 % HP), Trailblazer (moves leave puddles).

### B5. Later: abilities and natures as run rules (Arcana pattern)

Once D is done, a Lab option to pick a "rule" at run start from your Pokémon's ability pool
(Moxie: chain kills ramp damage; Guts: more damage under 50 %; Technician: weak moves +50 %;
Prankster: buffs fire from any slot). One pick reshapes what the cards mean.

   `G.LevelUp.offer` wrapper (before `card-rarity.js` so rarity paints it). For Lab endless, a

5. **Per-Pokémon contracts**: defs pushed into `G.MissionDefs` before `ensure` (`progress.js:58-62`),

   are coins for *that* Pokémon, which counters the 40.8k-per-Pokémon sink.

### C2. Medium effort

6. **Endless with a regime change** (decided, G6: Lab first; the normal-run choice comes later,
   once Lab endless is tuned). Lab first: after Eternatus, enemy HP ×1.25 per 2 minutes
   (cycle scaling, Vampire Survivors / Brotato), bosses continue (C1.1), affixes on, a **Judgment
   wave** every 5 minutes (one `judgment.js:376-383`-style legendary on the open map, doubling each

   past 30:00 (capped, like Megabonk's 8×). Normal runs later, as a choice at 30:00: "Face Arceus

   (`Player.heal`), mono-type pool (`G.Moves.poolFor`), elites from minute 0, double bosses, 3 slots
   (`pl.maxMoves` in a `startRun` wrapper), inverse type chart. 10-30 lines each.
8. **Hyper mode for maxed accounts** (doofcraft; answers upstream #256): clock ×2, Eternatus at
   15:00, 1.5× coins, own board. Decided (G9): yes, built as a seal preset, so it ships with C2.7.
9. **Pick your rift**: on approach, 3 type arenas; `G.Rift.debugNextType(type)` (`rift.js:303`)

10. **Alternative start kits** per Pokémon unlocked by contracts: override `G.startMoveOf` /
    `G.startMove2Of` (plain globals, `starters.js:61-76`; `start-move.js` already does this).

### C3. Higher effort

11. **Daily seeded run** with its own board: date seed via `shop.js:47-51`'s `today()`; the map is

12. **Biome threats**: wrap `G.Enemies.bagAt` to add biome candidates and hazard density per

13. **Quests with fixed, legible conditions** that unlock content (Megabonk/HoT): a move's
    "mastery" after 10 evolutions with a species → +7 % permanent to it and its TM in Kecleon.

## E. Workflow

Measured over 33 sessions, 2026-10-20 to 22 (UTC): 115 commits, 5,015 tool calls, 130 typed owner
messages (median 94 characters, 4 per working session), 90 Playwright checks. Where the time went:

- **Shared-checkout collisions**: one 22-minute stall waiting on "who builds", two rejected pushes,
  two merge conflicts, eleven cross-session messages spent coordinating. Every session runs in the
  same folder; `node fork/build.cjs` bundles the other session's half-done `dev.html`.
- **Repeated questions**: 20 AskUserQuestion batches, 1h44m of waiting; five were the same
  question ("should I push?") already answered the same way every time ("push each as it's done");
  three were Lab-vs-everyone scope, also answered. 17 commits sat unpushed until the owner returned.
- **Tests**: ~9 hours of wall time over three days (~16 min/session); `test-lab` ~2 min, full suite
  5-9 min; three known-flaky checks are run three times instead of fixed (`test-coop-background`,
  `test-coop` damage summary, `test-hp-above` timeout).
- **Compaction**: 8 compactions re-derive the same rules block each time (not the owner's cost, but
  a tax on long list sessions).
- **Not a cost**: permissions (auto mode; one rejected tool call in three days).
